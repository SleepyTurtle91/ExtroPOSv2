# Feature: Reports Reference

## Overview
Provides business intelligence and financial accounting summaries.

## Key Reports
- **Z-Report**: Daily sales closure summary (Tax, Payment Methods, Drawer Total).
- **Product Performance**: Analytics on top-selling items and categories.
- **SST Report**: Quarterly summary for tax filing.
- **Staff Commission**: Earnings report for commission-based service businesses.

## Data Source
Reports are generated by aggregating local Room data via `ReportingDao`.
